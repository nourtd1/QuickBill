# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Freelancers and solopreneurs — designers, developers, consultants, and independent service providers who run one-person operations. They bill clients on the go, track expenses by scanning receipts, and need everything handled in a few taps. They are often not finance experts; they want billing to feel fast and professional, not complicated.

## Product Purpose

QuickBill is a mobile-first invoicing and financial management tool. It lets solopreneurs create and send invoices, scan and log expenses, manage clients, track cash flow, and get AI-assisted insights — all from their phone. Success means a user can send a professional invoice in under 60 seconds and always know where their money stands.

## Brand Personality

Premium and polished. QuickBill should feel like the tool that makes freelancers look serious and competent to their clients — confident without being stiff, clean without being cold. Three words: **sharp, reliable, effortless**.

## Anti-references

- **QuickBooks / legacy accounting software**: dated, dense, corporate. The opposite of what QuickBill should be.
- **Fintech hype (Robinhood, crypto-adjacent apps)**: gamified, dark neon, speculative energy. QuickBill handles real work, not trading.
- **Generic SaaS template**: cream/sand hero + blue CTA, feels like it was AI-generated at scale. QuickBill must feel owned and intentional.
- **Bloated enterprise tools (SAP, Oracle)**: complex, cold, overwhelming. Everything billing software used to be.

## Design Principles

1. **Disappear into the task.** The interface should accelerate the user's work, not require their attention. Every tap should feel inevitable.
2. **Premium means precise.** Small decisions — spacing, type weight, icon choice — signal quality. Sloppiness is visible even to non-designers.
3. **Confidence without noise.** Color, motion, and emphasis should reinforce hierarchy and state, not decorate. One strong call-to-action per screen.
4. **Data earns trust.** Revenue numbers, client counts, growth metrics — present these with clarity and legibility first. The user's business depends on reading them correctly.
5. **Consistent everywhere.** A form field on the invoice screen should feel identical to one on the client screen. Familiarity is a feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA minimum for all interactive elements and text contrast.
- Support system font size scaling and reduced motion preferences.
- Multi-language support already in place (English, French) — maintain parity on new surfaces.
- Touch targets must meet the 44×44pt minimum throughout.
