## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: No banned patterns detected. However, three screens share identical decorative background templates and the font-black micro-text pattern creates visual monoculture.

**Deterministic scan**: Clean (0 findings).

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Contrast failures on secondary text
text-slate-400 on white yields ~3.0:1; placeholderTextColor="#CBD5E1" yields ~1.9:1. Fails WCAG AA across all screens.

### [P1] Typographic hierarchy collapse from weight overuse
font-black used for 70%+ of visible text. No weight gradient for scanning.

### [P2] Decorative background pattern repeated identically across 3 screens
Dashboard, Invoices, Clients use identical LinearGradient + floating circles template.

### [P2] Inconsistent component vocabulary
Border radius varies (24/28/32px) without semantic reason. Notification bell differs across 4 screens.

### [P2] Clients empty state is bare text with no actionable guidance
No icon, no CTA, no context. Breaks first-run experience.

## Persona Red Flags
